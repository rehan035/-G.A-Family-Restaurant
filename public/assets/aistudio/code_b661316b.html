The Heritage of Sialkot

Generations Gathered Over the Warmth of Authentic Copper Karahi

Nestled on the bustling arterial path of Kamanwala in Sialkot, Punjab, G.A Family Restaurant preserves the honored culinary traditions of aristocratic Mughal-Punjabi feasts. Every handi and karahi is cooked over roaring fires with pure desi ghee, fresh butchered local meats, and time-honored ground spices.

Designed purposefully for large family gatherings, private corporate dinners, and diaspora celebrations, our dining rooms offer private curtained family booths and grand dawat halls where memories are forged around fragrant platters.
